# Anum

**Anum** (from the Irish word for *soul* or *life*) is a 2D artificial life simulation framework. By combining physical dynamics - specifically anisotropic drag - with a flexible fuzzy logic cognitive architecture, Anum provides a rich environment for studying emergent biological phenomena.

## Key Features

* **Physics Engine with Anisotropic Drag:** Both the environment and individual cell bodies feature directional (anisotropic) drag. This creates non-trivial locomotion dynamics, making movement, orientation, and physical interaction key evolutionary constraints.
* **Fuzzy Logic Circuits:** Each cell’s genome encodes a fuzzy logic circuit mapping sensory inputs directly to motor and behavioural actuators. This allows for smooth, continuous control surfaces and nuanced behaviors.
* **Genetics & Evolution:** Genomes mutate, cross over, and adapt over generations based entirely on survival and reproductive success without a hardcoded fitness function.
* **Emergent Phenomena:** The open-ended design allows for the spontaneous evolution of complex ecological and social strategies, including:
  * **Multicellularity:** Cell adhesion, specialization, and coordinated movement.
  * **Sexual Reproduction:** Genetic exchange mechanisms and mate selection.
  * **Parasitism & Predation:** Resource theft, viral dynamics, and specialized predatory morphology.
  * **Communication & Language:** Emergent signalling networks between neighbouring cells or colonies.
